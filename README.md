# From Noise to Light

*A small online gallery about silence, prayer and the presence of God.*

**▶ Visit the gallery:** https://humbleinstrument.github.io/go-and-announce/

---

## About

Six scenes, one path: to stop, to be still, and to let God speak.

A man lost in the noise of the city closes the door, sits in silence and places himself in the presence of God. Something changes within. The world outside stays the same, but he no longer sees it the same way.

Each scene comes with a verse from the Gospel and a short story.

| # | Scene | Verse |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Noise | Matthew 11:28 |
| II | Stopping | Matthew 6:6 |
| III | The Silence | Mark 1:35 |
| IV | The Presence | John 15:4 |
| V | The Light | John 8:12 |
| VI | Back to the World | Matthew 5:14, 16 |
